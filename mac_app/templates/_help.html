Help

Getting started

Turn the wand on and press Connect wand. The Mac looks for it over Bluetooth for up to 10 seconds.

To try Grimoire without the wand, press Use simulated wand. A toolbar at the bottom of the page then casts for you.

Casting

Hold the cast button, draw a gesture in the air, and let go. A cast lasts at most 3 seconds. The 3D view draws the path of the wand's tip as you move, seen from behind your hand.

It doesn't matter how far the wand is twisted in your hand, as long as the tip isn't pointing almost straight up or down when you start. Up and down in the room still count: an up-down stroke is not a sideways one.

The status at the right of the top bar says where the wand is: ready, casting, or waiting for the Mac's answer. It is ready for the next cast as soon as the answer arrives.

Reading a result

The name is the gesture whose examples the cast came closest to. The bar under it shows how close: the mark is the cast's distance, the line is the rejection threshold. A cast beyond the line counts as Unknown.

The two charts show what the wand measured during the cast. Drag across one to zoom in; double-click it to zoom back out.

Training a gesture

Open Dataset. Pick a gesture, press Start recording, perform the gesture once, then press Stop and save. A recording stops by itself after 5 seconds.

Record five or more examples of each gesture, made the way you will cast it. Check dataset compares each example with all the others and points out the ones that look like another gesture; delete those. Clear dataset starts over, and keeps the files in the cleared folder.

Settings

The rejection threshold is how far a cast may be from its nearest example and still count. Lower is stricter: fewer wrong answers, more Unknown.

Each gesture's square is the colour the app shows it in: its trail in the 3D view, and the block behind its name under Last cast. Its menu is what the Mac does: show a notification, send a web request, or switch something in the living room. Add a gesture by its name, then record examples of it. Save settings to apply.

Living room

A room to try gestures on. In Settings, choose Switch the lamp, or the door, TV or curtains, for a gesture. Casting it then switches that device. Press a device to switch it by hand. Each time the app starts, the room starts with everything off and closed.

Keys

Esc closes a panel, or a question in one.
